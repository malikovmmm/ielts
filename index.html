Four papers, four preparation tracks

30 minutes, 40 questions. We train note-taking, spotting distractors and following accents from the UK, Australia and North America.

Reading

60 minutes, 3 long texts. You learn skimming, scanning and a time plan of 20 minutes per passage.

Writing
